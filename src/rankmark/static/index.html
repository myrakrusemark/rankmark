Each hidden bit is the parity of the chosen word's rank. Durability adds error-correction so the message survives edits and truncation, at the cost of needing more text per copy. Wording variety is the sampling temperature: 0 always takes the model's top choice (cleanest watermark, but small models can loop); higher samples more widely, which breaks loops and reads more naturally — the decode is identical either way. The AI keeps writing until at least one full copy is embedded, then stops on its own.
